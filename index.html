About

Seven years of Jacksonville BI experience, now working for you

Hi, I'm Michael, founder of T7 Technology Solutions. I'm a Microsoft Certified data professional with more than seven years in business intelligence, and I've spent that time in Jacksonville turning slow, manual processes into automated reports and workflows.

At a large maritime and logistics company, I built payroll, HR and U.S. Coast Guard compliance reporting for a workforce of 3,600+ employees. Since then I've consulted for a mid-sized healthcare software company and a large multi-site education and social services nonprofit, automating everything from client reports to the monthly financial close.

Most recently, I've worked with a large global medical technology manufacturer. I kept their sales-compensation and request-tracking apps running smoothly through a company-wide move to a new business system, automated purchase-order collection across regions so it no longer depended on a single person, and built executive reporting that tracks products from launch to exit, showing where inventory and margin are at risk.

Before data, I taught algebra, so explaining numbers in plain English is second nature. I use modern AI tools to build faster, but every project starts with your problem and your numbers, not the technology.

When I'm not building automations, you'll find me in the woodshop, outdoors, or with my family. That's the point of all this: less time on busywork, more time for what matters.
